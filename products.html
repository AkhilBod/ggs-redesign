Featured build: product

Brisqo, the 24/7 AI front desk.

A multi-tenant agentic voice platform that answers the phone for local service businesses, and the funnel that signs them up.

  • Voice AI, transcription and n8n workflows on Railway, wired to Google Calendar, Twilio SMS and a Postgres tenant registry.
  • CLI and n8n admin tooling to onboard and manage client businesses, with single- and multi-tenant call-forwarding failover so the AI answers only when the real front desk doesn't.
  • Templated across 10 verticals, from dental and HVAC to law firms.
  • Full customer funnel: an ebook-gated lead magnet with attribution, an admin dashboard that tracks leads from New to Won, and a self-serve signup that turns a plan choice into a signed SaaS service agreement with ESIGN-compliant e-signature.

Built with Vapi, Deepgram, n8n, Twilio, Postgres, Google Calendar, Claude and Railway.

Featured build: platform, Sep 2026 to present

Provenant AI, B2B leads from public records.

A prospecting platform that starts from free government records instead of scraped lists, and shows its work on every lead.

25+public datasets
16M+records
~500automated tests
100%claims linked to a source
  • Pulls 25+ public datasets and checks each company's own website against its licensed phone, license number and address.
  • Claude explains why a business fits AI receptionists and automation, with every claim linked to its source.
  • Duplicate resolution with a human review queue, explainable versioned scores, and "why now" triggers from dated public records.
  • A one-page AI opportunity audit that doubles as a lead magnet, plus a REST API and CSV export.

Built with Next.js, Postgres and Claude. About 500 tests, run in CI.
