Insurance Policy Visibility PlatformPilotPrivate deployment, by enquiry

A single view of a global insurance book

An international broking group places risk through local partners, in eight currencies, with the master record in a policy administration system whose reports have to be exported and rebuilt in a spreadsheet before they answer anything. The platform consolidates policy, premium, commission, renewal and receivables data into one normalised store, converts every amount to a single base currency, and serves it through purpose-built report views with an AI layer on top.

  • Scheduled read-only sync from the policy system of record, validated, paginated and safe to re-run
  • Policy PDFs ingested and field-extracted with per-field confidence scoring, routed to human review below threshold
  • Every amount normalised to one base currency, with the rate and its as-of date stamped onto the record
  • Seven report views: book in force and pipeline, renewals due, partner and carrier performance, commission received and ageing
  • An AI layer that writes the portfolio briefing, answers plain-English questions against live data, and flags renewal risk and premium anomalies

Built and operated by Golden Gate Solutions LLC. Deployed privately per client, containerised and cloud-neutral, with read-only access to the source system by design.

Provenant AIPilotFrom public record to pipeline

B2B prospecting that starts with the public record

Prospect lists are bought, not built: the same stale rows everyone else has, with no way to tell why a name is on the list. Provenant AI builds the list from free government records instead, then finds each company's own website, verifies it belongs to them, and reads it. Every fact on screen carries the record it came from.

  • Builds prospects from state and city licence files, business registries, the CMS provider registry, the FMCSA carrier census and the IRS nonprofit file
  • Accepts a website only when the licensed phone, licence number or address verifies it, then extracts contacts, services, hours, booking and chat
  • Turns dated records into named sales triggers, each traceable to a record and a date
  • Explainable, versioned scores that break into parts showing their evidence
  • Lead lists, CRM-ready CSV export, REST API, webhooks, and a human review queue
Request access

Provenant AI sends nothing: no email, no texts, no calls. It prepares evidence-backed lists; outreach happens in your own tools. The crawler identifies itself and obeys robots.txt.
